About Our Application

Power of the Word is a Bahá'í quotes application that displays inspirational writings with home screen widgets and automatic rotation features. The Application is available on both iOS (Apple App Store) and Android (Google Play Store) platforms.

Our Application displays Bahá'í quotes sourced from BahaiQuotes.com (used with permission) and includes features such as favorites, search functionality, theme customization, and synchronized home screen widgets.
